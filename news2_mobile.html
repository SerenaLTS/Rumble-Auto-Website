RUMBLE AUTO NEWS

Inside the RUMBLE Auto workshop.

Our Arndell Park team at work keeping SITRAK trucks serviced, inspected and ready for the road.

Category Workshop Update Location Arndell Park, NSW Published August 2026

Every truck that enters our workshop is supported by a team focused on careful workmanship, practical technical knowledge and getting operators back on the road.

These recent workshop moments show the range of work carried out by the RUMBLE Auto team—from scheduled servicing and driveline repairs to pre-delivery inspections, accessory fitment and final vehicle preparation.

Service and repairs, handled in-house

Our technicians support SITRAK operators with scheduled servicing, detailed inspections and major mechanical work. Recent jobs include replacing a clutch assembly and flywheel on a C7H Prime Mover, along with scheduled servicing for another C7H.

Prepared for work, from inspection to handover

Before a vehicle leaves RUMBLE Auto, our team carries out the checks and preparation needed for its next job. That includes a thorough pre-delivery inspection of the drivetrain on a G5S 4x2 Curtainsider and a final clean before customer handover.
