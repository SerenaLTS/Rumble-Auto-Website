Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how RUMBLE Auto uses cookies, local storage, analytics, advertising measurement and third-party website services.

Effective date: 19 October 2026

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, including local storage, can remember preferences, support website functionality and help measure how a website is used.

2. How We Use Cookies

RUMBLE Auto uses cookies and similar technologies to:

  • operate and secure the website;
  • remember your cookie consent choice;
  • understand website traffic and navigation where analytics consent is provided;
  • measure website performance and enquiry activity; and
  • diagnose technical issues and prevent misuse.

3. Essential Website Storage

Essential browser storage remembers your separate analytics and advertising preferences in your browser. Older analytics-only choices do not grant advertising consent; the updated notice asks you to choose again. The consent preference does not contain information entered in an enquiry form. Form handling may also use temporary session storage to prevent duplicate submissions.

4. Analytics Cookies

Our website uses Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to help us understand page visits, traffic patterns, device types, referral sources, downloads and interactions with links or forms. Analytics storage is denied by default. Choose Analytics only or Accept all to allow it, or Reject optional to keep it denied.

Analytics information is used for aggregated website performance reporting and to improve website content and customer pathways rather than to identify individual visitors.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Content

With advertising consent, we may use Google Ads and Meta Pixel to measure successful enquiries and support advertising personalisation. Enquiry type and a unique event identifier may be shared for measurement. Where enhanced matching is enabled, contact details supplied with a successful enquiry may be normalised and securely hashed before sharing with Google or Meta. Hashing supports matching and does not make the information anonymous. Advertising storage and advertising user data remain denied unless you select Accept all.

Pages on this website may include Google Maps, embedded video, externally hosted images, PDF brochure links, social media links, form handling or other third-party services. These providers may receive technical information and use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. The choices in our cookie notice control our analytics and advertising consent settings; it may not control technologies used by third-party content that you choose to open or interact with.

6. Managing Cookies

You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to delete existing cookies and site data, block future cookies or receive a warning before cookies are stored. Blocking cookies or storage may affect consent preferences, embedded content or other website functionality.

7. Consent Choices

Choose Reject optional to decline analytics and advertising, Analytics only to allow analytics without advertising, or Accept all to allow both. Closing the notice declines optional tracking. You can reopen Cookie settings in the footer and change or withdraw your choice at any time. Withdrawal changes future tracking permissions; it does not undo information already sent or necessarily delete previously stored cookies. You can delete cookies using your browser settings.

8. Personal Information

Some cookie, analytics or technical information may be considered personal information if it can identify or reasonably be linked to an individual. For more information about how RUMBLE Auto handles personal information, please read our Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in our website, tools, business operations, consent practices or legal requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy or how cookies are used on our website, contact RUMBLE Auto at info@rumbleauto.com.au, call (02) 9621 1886, or write to 15 Contaplas St, Arndell Park NSW 2148, Australia.
